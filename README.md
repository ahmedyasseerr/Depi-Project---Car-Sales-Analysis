## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an end-to-end data analysis solution developed as part of the **Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI)** under the **Junior Data Analyst** track. 

The primary objective is to analyze a comprehensive car sales dataset to uncover market trends, examine vehicle pricing factors, and evaluate sales performance across various states, providing actionable insights for business decision-makers.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- **Top Performers:** Identify the highest-selling car brands and models.
- **Valuation & Depreciation:** Analyze the impact of vehicle age and mileage (odometer) on the selling price.
- **Market Price Comparison:** Compare actual selling prices against estimated market values (MMR) to evaluate profit margins and discounts.
- **Interactive Reporting:** Deliver an intuitive, user-friendly Power BI dashboard for stakeholders.
